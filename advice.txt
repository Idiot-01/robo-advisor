This analysis of the mNAV (Modified Net Asset Value) for MicroStrategy (MSTR) is based on the time-series data provided, spanning from April 2025 to May 2026.

### 1. What the current mNAV value (0.9748) signifies
The mNAV ratio represents the relationship between the company’s market capitalization and the value of its underlying assets (primarily its Bitcoin holdings).

*   **Trading at a Discount:** A value of **0.9748** signifies that MicroStrategy is currently trading at a **2.5% discount** to its Net Asset Value. 
*   **The "Sats-per-share" value:** Effectively, by buying the stock at this mNAV, you are acquiring the underlying Bitcoin for less than the current market price of Bitcoin itself, essentially getting the company's core software business for "free" or at a negative valuation.
*   **Historical Context:** For MSTR, an mNAV below 1.0 is statistically rare. Historically, MSTR has traded at a premium (often 1.5x to 2.5x) due to its intelligent use of leverage and its status as a spot-BTC proxy for institutional portfolios.
