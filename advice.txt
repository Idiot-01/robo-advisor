Based on the time-series data provided, here is the analysis of MicroStrategy's (MSTR) **mNAV** (Market-to-Net Asset Value). 

*Note: mNAV typically represents the ratio of MSTR’s market capitalization to the value of the Bitcoin it holds. A value of 1.0 means the market values MSTR exactly at the price of its BTC holdings.*

---

### 1. What the current mNAV value (0.8798) signifies
The most recent data point (May 27, 2026) shows an **mNAV of 0.8798**.

*   **Trading at a Discount:** This signifies that MSTR is currently trading at roughly a **12% discount** to its underlying Bitcoin holdings. 
*   **Market Sentiment:** Usually, MSTR trades at a premium (mNAV > 1.0) because of its intelligent debt structure and its status as a leveraged BTC play. An mNAV below 1.0 suggests extreme bearishness toward the company, concerns about its debt/liabilities, or a lack of demand for "wrapped" Bitcoin products (potentially due to the success of Spot BTC ETFs).
*   **Historical Context:** In this dataset, the mNAV has collapsed from a high of **2.02** (May 2025) to the current **0.87**. The "value proposition" of the stock has shifted from a premium growth engine to a distressed asset relative to the BTC on its balance sheet.
