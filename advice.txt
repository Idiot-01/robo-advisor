Based on the time-series data provided, here is an analysis of the mNAV (Modified Net Asset Value) for MSTR.

### 1. What the current mNAV value signifies
The current mNAV value (as of June 5, 2026) is **0.8622**.

In the context of MicroStrategy (MSTR), the NAV ratio typically represents the relationship between the company's market capitalization and the value of its underlying Bitcoin holdings.
*   **mNAV > 1.0:** The stock is trading at a **premium**. Investors are paying more for the stock than the literal value of the Bitcoin it holds (often due to leverage, tax benefits, or ease of access).
*   **mNAV = 1.0:** The stock is trading at **par**. The market value equals the underlying asset value.
*   **mNAV < 1.0:** The stock is trading at a **discount**. 

**Significance:** At **0.8622**, MSTR is currently trading at an approximately **13.8% discount** to its underlying assets. This suggests that the market is currently undervaluing MSTR's holdings, or there is a lack of confidence/liquidity in the stock relative to the spot price of the assets it holds.
