Based on the time-series data provided (running from April 2025 through May 2026), here is an analysis of the mNAV (Market-to-Net Asset Value) for MicroStrategy (MSTR).

### 1. What the current mNAV value signifies
The current value (as of May 20, 2026) is **0.8935**.

In the context of MicroStrategy, the mNAV represents the ratio of the company's market capitalization to the value of the Bitcoin it holds.
*   **A value of 1.0** means the stock is trading exactly at the value of its Bitcoin holdings (par).
*   **A value > 1.0** signifies a "premium," where investors are paying extra for the company’s software business, its ability to use leverage to buy more Bitcoin, or its status as a regulated ETF alternative.
*   **A value < 1.0** signifies a "discount."

**Significance:** At **0.8935**, the market is currently valuing MSTR at a **~10.6% discount** to its Bitcoin holdings. This suggests that for every $1.00 of Bitcoin MicroStrategy owns, the stock market is only "paying" roughly 89 cents. This is a significant shift from the start of your data set, where it traded at a massive premium.
