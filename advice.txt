### 1. What the Current mNAV Value Signifies
**Current Value (June 13, 2026): 0.8165**

In the context of MicroStrategy, mNAV (often referred to as the "NAV Premium") represents the ratio of the company’s Market Capitalization to the value of its Bitcoin holdings.
*   **A value of 1.00** means the market is pricing MSTR exactly at the value of its Bitcoin.
*   **A value > 1.00** means the stock is trading at a premium (investors are paying extra for the company’s software business, its ability to stack more BTC, or its intelligent use of leverage).
*   **A value < 1.00** means the stock is trading at a **discount**.

**Significance:** At **0.8165**, MSTR is trading at an approximately **18.3% discount** to its underlying Bitcoin holdings. This suggests that the market is currently "undervaluing" the company relative to the digital assets on its balance sheet. This is historically rare for MSTR, which typically trades at a premium.
