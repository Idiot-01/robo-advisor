### 1. What the current mNAV value signifies
The most recent value (2026-05-18) is **0.9887**.

*   **At par with assets:** An mNAV of 1.0 means the market capitalization of the company is exactly equal to the value of its Bitcoin holdings (plus its software business).
*   **The "Discount" Phase:** At **0.9887**, MSTR is trading at a very slight **discount (roughly 1.1%)** to its underlying assets. Effectively, you are buying Bitcoin through the stock at a price slightly cheaper than the spot market price, while getting the software business and the company's "intelligent leverage" for free.
*   **Historical Context:** Historically, MSTR has frequently traded at a **premium** (mNAV of 1.5 to 2.5). Trading below 1.0 is statistically rare for this stock and usually indicates a period of extreme market pessimism or "de-leveraging" by equity investors.
