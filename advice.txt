**Current mNAV (June 15, 2026): 0.7888**

The mNAV represents the ratio of the company's Market Capitalization to its Net Asset Value (primarily its Bitcoin holdings).
*   **A Discount to NAV:** An mNAV of **0.7888** signifies that MSTR is trading at a **~21% discount** to the value of the assets on its balance sheet. 
*   **Historical Context:** For MicroStrategy, this is a highly unusual and "cheap" valuation. Historically, MSTR has traded at a significant premium (often 1.5x to 2.5x NAV) because it provides leveraged exposure to Bitcoin. Trading below 1.0 means the market is currently valuing the operating business and its "intelligent leverage" at a negative value, or it is pricing in extreme fear regarding the company's debt obligations.
