This analysis is based on the provided mNAV (modified Net Asset Value) time-series for MicroStrategy (MSTR), spanning from April 2025 to late May 2026.

### 1. What the Current mNAV Value Signifies
The final data point (May 25, 2026) is **0.8642**.

In the context of MSTR, mNAV typically represents the ratio of the company’s market valuation relative to the value of its Bitcoin holdings.
*   **mNAV > 1.0:** The stock is trading at a **premium**. Investors are paying more for the stock than the underlying Bitcoin is worth (often due to leverage, ease of access in brokerage accounts, or expected future BTC purchases).
*   **mNAV < 1.0:** The stock is trading at a **discount**. The market is valuing the company at less than the "spot" value of its Bitcoin holdings.

**Significance:** At **0.8642**, MicroStrategy is currently trading at a **~13.6% discount** to its Bitcoin holdings. This suggests that the market has become pessimistic about the stock's ability to command a premium, or is pricing in concerns regarding the company’s debt load or potential equity dilution.
